Judgment framework / 判断框架 · locked / 锁定

For loss-making companies, examine cash runway; for profitable companies, examine whether operating cash flow covers capital expenditure. Clean-energy businesses face long financing cycles and material scale-up commitments, so historical positive cash flow alone does not prove funding sufficiency.
